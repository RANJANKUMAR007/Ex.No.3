**Date:** 03/08/2026

# Aim

To understand and implement different Prompt Engineering techniques by designing prompts for an AI-powered customer support chatbot. The chatbot is capable of assisting customers with product troubleshooting, order tracking, and answering general inquiries while maintaining a friendly, accurate, and conversational interaction. The effectiveness of each prompting technique is analyzed based on the quality of the generated responses.

---

# Use Case

## AI-Powered Customer Support Chatbot

### Scenario

A company wants to develop an AI-powered chatbot that provides instant support to customers. The chatbot should be able to understand customer queries, identify product-related issues, provide troubleshooting guidance, track customer orders, answer frequently asked questions, and transfer complex issues to a human support representative whenever necessary. The chatbot should communicate naturally, reduce customer waiting time, and improve the overall customer service experience.

---

# 1. Straightforward Prompt

## Prompt

```text
Design an AI-powered chatbot that helps customers resolve product issues, track their orders, answer frequently asked questions, and provide a friendly conversational experience.
```

## Generated Response

The AI-powered chatbot is designed to provide real-time customer support by understanding user queries and responding with accurate information. It assists customers in troubleshooting common product issues through step-by-step guidance, retrieves order tracking details using order IDs, answers frequently asked questions related to products and services, and escalates unresolved issues to a human support agent. The chatbot maintains a polite, conversational, and user-friendly tone throughout the interaction, ensuring customers receive quick and reliable assistance.

### Evaluation

- The prompt is clear and direct.
- The AI understands the objective without additional context.
- Suitable for generating concise and accurate responses.
- Best used when the required output is straightforward and specific.

---

# 2. Tabular Format Prompting

## Prompt

```text
Present the major features of the AI-powered customer support chatbot in a table containing Feature, Purpose, and Example.
```

## Generated Response

| Feature | Purpose | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Product Troubleshooting | Identifies and resolves common product issues | Suggests restarting the device or checking network settings |
| Order Tracking | Provides real-time delivery updates | Displays the current shipping status of an order |
| Frequently Asked Questions | Answers common customer queries | Explains return policy, warranty details, and payment methods |
| Human Support Escalation | Transfers unresolved issues to support staff | Connects the customer to a live support representative |
| Personalized Assistance | Uses customer information for customized responses | Greets users by name and remembers previous interactions |

### Evaluation

- Organizes information in a structured format.
- Makes feature comparison simple and easy to understand.
- Improves readability for technical documentation.
- Suitable for reports, documentation, and presentations.

---

# 3. Preceding Question Prompting

## Prompt

```text
Why is understanding customer intent important for an AI-powered chatbot? Explain how the chatbot uses this understanding to provide product troubleshooting, order tracking, and general customer support.
```

## Generated Response

Understanding customer intent enables the chatbot to identify the actual problem before generating a response. By analyzing the user's request, the chatbot can determine whether the customer requires technical troubleshooting, order status information, or answers to general questions. Once the intent is identified, the chatbot provides personalized solutions, retrieves relevant information, and guides the customer through the required steps. If the issue cannot be solved automatically, it seamlessly transfers the conversation to a human support representative. This approach improves response accuracy, enhances customer satisfaction, and creates a more natural conversational experience.

### Evaluation

- Produces detailed and context-aware responses.
- Encourages logical reasoning before answering.
- Generates more informative explanations than direct prompting.
- Suitable for analytical and descriptive tasks.

---

# 4. Missing Word Prompting

## Prompt

```text
Complete the following sentences.

1. AI-powered chatbots improve customer __________.
2. Customers can check their __________ status through the chatbot.
3. Product issues are solved using step-by-step __________.
4. Difficult customer requests are transferred to a __________ support representative.
5. Friendly communication improves the overall customer __________.
```

## Generated Response

```text
1. satisfaction
2. order
3. troubleshooting
4. human
5. experience
```

### Evaluation

- Evaluates the AI's contextual understanding.
- Useful for educational and assessment-based applications.
- Simple prompts can effectively test language comprehension.
- Helps reinforce important concepts related to the topic.

# Comparison of Prompt Engineering Techniques

| Prompt Type | Purpose | Strengths | Best Use Case |
|--------------|---------|-----------|---------------|
| Straightforward Prompt | Provides direct instructions | Quick, simple, and accurate responses | Simple question-answering tasks |
| Tabular Format Prompting | Organizes information into tables | Easy comparison and improved readability | Documentation and technical reports |
| Preceding Question Prompting | Builds context before generating responses | Produces detailed and logical explanations | Analytical and reasoning-based tasks |
| Missing Word Prompting | Tests contextual understanding | Simple method for evaluating AI knowledge | Educational exercises and assessments |

---

# Conclusion

Prompt Engineering plays a significant role in improving the quality and effectiveness of AI-generated responses. Different prompting techniques produce different styles of outputs depending on the nature of the task. Straightforward prompts are suitable for obtaining direct answers, while tabular prompting organizes information efficiently. Preceding question prompting improves reasoning by encouraging the AI to understand the context before responding, and missing word prompting evaluates contextual understanding through fill-in-the-blank exercises. Selecting the appropriate prompting technique enables AI systems to generate more accurate, informative, and user-friendly responses for real-world applications such as customer support chatbots.

---

# Result

Thus, the various Prompt Engineering techniques, namely Straightforward Prompting, Tabular Format Prompting, Preceding Question Prompting, and Missing Word Prompting, were successfully implemented and evaluated using the AI-powered customer support chatbot use case. The generated responses demonstrated the effectiveness of each prompting technique in improving communication, organization, reasoning, and contextual understanding.
